# Primitives

AGenNext is governed by 18 primitives. Every primitive must be identity-bound, domain-scoped, relation-connected, path-governed, policy-enforced, trust-calculated, wallet-aware where needed, agent-operable, tool/model/data/device aware, port-bound, kube-deliverable, openbox-sealable, exchange-ready, evidence-backed, and lifecycle-managed.

Anything may work inside the platform, but everything must follow the rules, add value, preserve the Fabric, prove outcomes, and continuously improve to remain alive.

## What each primitive owns

### Identity
= who or what exists

### Domain
= where it belongs and what scope it serves

### Relation
= who or what is connected to whom or what, under which domain and rule

Relation creates structure. Without Relation, the graph only describes disconnected nodes.

### Path
= the allowed route through the Fabric from intent to outcome

A path is made of relations, gates, ports, and trust checks. Path creates movement. Without Path, no action reaches completion.

### Policy
= what rules apply

### Trust
= whether it may continue

### Wallet
= what it can carry, receive, prove, or settle

### Agent
= what can observe, decide, act, report, and reconcile

### Tool
= what capability can be invoked

### Model
= what intelligence can be used

### Data
= what information can be read, written, streamed, or transformed

### Device
= what physical or edge thing can participate

### Port
= where anything enters, exits, or crosses

### Kube
= what runs and delivers the promise

### OpenBox
= what is sealed, transported, replayed, and verified

### Exchange
= what value, capability, benefit, access, or outcome moves

### Evidence
= what proves the action, decision, value, and outcome

### Lifecycle
= how everything is created, improved, suspended, retired, or banished
