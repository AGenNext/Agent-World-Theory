# Policy

Policy is a rule that governs what can happen inside a world.

## Definition

A policy controls access, approvals, tool use, data handling, execution, safety, and shutdown. Policies are first-class objects in the Fabric.

## Policy types

- Access policy: who may act
- Approval policy: what requires human gate
- Tool policy: what tools may be used
- Data policy: what data may be accessed
- Execution policy: how actions may run
- Safety policy: what blocks execution
- Shutdown policy: what triggers suspension

## Policy and primitives

Every primitive is policy-governed. Policy gates every relation and path traversal in the Fabric.
