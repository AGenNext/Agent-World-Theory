## Agent Safety

Agentic systems can cause unintended, unsafe, costly, or harmful effects if implemented without proper controls.

Any implementation must include appropriate safeguards, including but not limited to:

- Human approval
- Identity and access control
- Audit logging
- Traceability
- Policy enforcement
- Sandbox testing
- Data protection
- Kill switch or shutdown controls
- Monitoring and incident response
